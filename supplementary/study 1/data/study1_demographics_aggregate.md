# Study 1 aggregate demographics

- N = 20
- Gender: 10 women, 10 men
- Age range: 18–43 years
- Mean age: 23.65 years
- SD age: 6.98 years
- Broad background: 11 design-related, 9 non-design-related
- Handedness: 17 right-handed, 3 left-handed

Exact age, detailed department, and handedness are intentionally not released at participant level
because the sample is small and these quasi-identifiers can increase re-identification risk.

The participant codes in the subjective-rating source workbooks are internally consistent across the
empty and crowded conditions. They are **not assumed to correspond row-for-row** to the P01–P20
codes in the legacy standalone demographics CSV, whose ordering differs.
